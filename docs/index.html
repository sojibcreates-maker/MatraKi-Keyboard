Key Features

Everything a modern Bangla keyboard should be

Legacy keyboards stopped evolving years ago. MatraKi rebuilds Bangla input from the engine up — smarter vocabulary, real grammar, and productivity tools right inside your keyboard.

🧠

AI Language Intelligence

38,592+ pre-trained words with smart agglutinative suffix stemming — automatically recognizes 225,000+ inflected forms like -গুলো, -দের, -টা, -কে.

🔮

Next-Word Prediction

Context-aware prediction with 130+ conversational bigram contexts. Press Tab to accept natural follow-up words as you type.

👤

Subject-Verb Concordance

Detects subject pronouns and re-ranks candidates — আমি করব, তুমি করবে, আপনি করবেন — automatically correct.

📖

Bangla Academy Grammar

Auto-fixes ণ-ত্ব, ষ-ত্ব and legacy spelling errors: সান্তনা → সান্ত্বনা, পুরষ্কার → পুরস্কার, শারিরীক → শারীরিক.

🧮

Live Math & Unit Converter

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I switch between English and Bangla?

Press Ctrl + Space or configure your preferred Fcitx5 hotkey in settings. You can also click the floating MatraKi TopBar pill to toggle layouts.

Does MatraKi work on Wayland?

Yes! MatraKi uses the native Fcitx5 input method protocol with first-class support on GNOME, KDE Plasma, Deepin, Hyprland and Sway — as well as X11.

Can I add my own words and shortcuts?

Absolutely. Open Settings → Dictionary to search 38,592+ entries and add custom phonetic patterns with live preview. Custom snippets go in ~/.local/share/matraki-keyboard/snippets.json.

Which keyboard layouts are supported?

Avro Phonetic (AI Enhanced), Probhat, National (Jatiya), Munir Optima, Borno, and Avro Easy.
